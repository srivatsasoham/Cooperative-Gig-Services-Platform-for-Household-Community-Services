Cooperative Gig Services Platform for Household & Community Services

Transitioning India's gig economy from centralized algorithmic extraction to a self-sustaining, democratic worker-owned cooperative ecosystem.

The Centralized Gig Crisis

  • 20% to 35% Extractive Cuts: Corporate middlemen siphon massive commissions out of workers' manual labor.
  • Algorithmic Oppression & Shadow Banning: Opaque algorithms penalize workers arbitrarily with zero right to appeal.
  • Zero Social Security: Gig workers lack health insurance, pension funds, or emergency accident coverage.
  • Capital Flight: Profits generated in Indian neighborhoods are routed to external corporate entities rather than circulating in the local community.

The SahakariGig Revolution

  • 97% Direct Take-Home: Workers receive 97% of every rupee paid. Only 3% is retained for the worker welfare fund.
  • Democratic DAO Co-op Governance: Workers vote on platform policies, dispute tribunals, and commission rates (1 member = 1 vote).
  • Built-in Safety Net & Tool Bank: Health insurance, emergency accident grants, and free tool depot access funded by the 3% pool.
  • Patronage Dividends: 100% of platform surplus profit is returned quarterly to workers and community members.
